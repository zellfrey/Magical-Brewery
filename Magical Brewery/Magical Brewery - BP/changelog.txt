v0.5 - FULL CHANGELOG(SPOILERS)
Added:
    - Brewer's Desk. item, block, crafting bench A new research station fitting for a magical brewer
        - Seals are now crafted using the Brewer's Desk
        - Tome pages can now be crafted in the Brewers Desk
    - Brewing Research
        - Singular Ingredient & Potion Research, e.g Healing, Strength, Swiftness
        - Catalyzer research(this includes; glowstone, redstone, gunpowder e.t.c)
        - Cask Oddity research(You can figure this one out for yourself)
        - "Multi Clue" Ingredient & Potion Research, e.g Harming
    - Potion Vessel Chapters
        - Amethyst Vessels
        - Glass Vessels
    - 3 new Cask Seals; item, block, recipe, tome pages & chapters
        - Seal of Inspiration
        - Seal of Memories
        - Seal of Expansion
    - A new Cask variant(this includes the planks and slabs)
        - Cask of Decay, Stained Planks of Decay, Stained Slabs of Decay, Decay potion variants
    - A new budding crystal, buds, cluster, seed & dust
        - Echo growing crystal
        - Echo seed
        - Echo Buds/Cluster
        - Echo Dust
    - Amethyst bottles
    - Amethyst Potions of ALL minecraft Effects(except lingering... I'll get to it eventually)
        - redstone variant
        - glowstone variant
        - pure redstone variant
        - pure glowstone variant
        - echo variant
        - gunpowder variant
    - Amethyst Potion cask aging compatibility
    - Splash Potion cask aging FINALLY
    - xlong & xstrong Splash potion variants
    - A new structure can be found in the caves below, along with a new short story
Updated:
    - Fixed growing crystal amethyst texture
    - Optimised budding crystal growth
    - Planks of Weakness block has correct sound(stone -> wood)
    - Cask tasting will show an additional message depending on certain interactions
    - Budding Crystal loottables changed to just check for a pickaxe tag, i.e other addon pickaxes are now compatible
    - Art pass to ALL Seals
    - Glassmith hammer can now be used to mine out budding crystals & is an alternative to Silk touch. 
    - Refactored potion consuming mechanic
    - Glass potions now have a limited number of effects that it can hold
    - Moved xlong & xstrong glass potions to vanilla glass potions category
    - Some seals can now be effected by the type of potionliquid/deliveryType(e.g splash potion or lingering potion)
    - Brewing pages recipe changed
Author comment:
I think I have covered everything. If I missed anything then I apologies. This update has been a long journey and it's
about time that I get something out of the door before this addon starts growing mould. I really hope you enjoy this
new update. Not only does this update cover basically every aspect of the addon/mod, but it is also 2x the size of
0.4.
